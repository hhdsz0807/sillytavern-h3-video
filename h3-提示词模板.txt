MiniMax H3 全参考模式（Ref2VA）官方六段格式 · 模板
来源：github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3/skills/h3-prompt-writing/references/ref-en.txt

用法：放进角色卡描述 / 世界书 / 作者注释，让模型在正文后补一个 [[H3]]…[[/H3]] 块；
或者直接点扩展面板的「按正文生成六段」（会自动按这个格式写）。

六段顺序固定：subject_definitions → summary → retention_analysis → detailed_description → overall_soundscape → non_diegetic_music

================ 骨架 ================
[[H3]]
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the woman in <Picture 1>, with long dark hair and a light-blue cardigan.
<Subject 2> is the man in <Picture 2>, with short black hair and a grey jacket.
<Subject 3> is the environment in <Picture 3>, a window-side booth with pale wooden partitions and late-afternoon light.

summary:
[reference generation] The target video shows <Subject 1> and <Subject 2> inside <Subject 3> in a single quiet exchange.

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): fully_preserved - identity, hair and clothing are retained.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - identity and clothing are retained.
<Subject 3> (appears in [Shot 1], [Shot 2]): fully_preserved - furniture, props and lighting are retained.

detailed_description:
The target video uses a flat 2D anime cel style with warm late-afternoon window light.
[Shot 1] A medium two-shot across the table inside <Subject 3>. <Subject 2> sits on screen right facing <Subject 1>. He looks down, then up, and says, <d>[Chinese] 抱歉。</d>
[Shot 2] At 00:05.500, the shot cuts to a close-up of <Subject 1> from the front. She forces a small smile and answers, <d>[Chinese] 不要道歉啊。</d> She looks down at her hands.

overall_soundscape:
Quiet restaurant room tone with soft ice-shifting sounds and one chair scrape.

non_diegetic_music:
N/A
[[/H3]]
================ 骨架结束 ================

官方硬规则（来自 ref-en.txt）：
1. 六段全英文；只有台词、歌词、画面可见文字保留原语言，写在 <d>[Chinese] …</d> 里。
2. subject_definitions：要被复用的可见内容（人物/场景/服装道具）都定义成 <Subject N>；参考图只用于定义角色/场景/服装时，把来源写进该 Subject，**不要**单独写 <Picture N> 行；只有某图本身是某镜首帧/关键帧/尾帧/构图锚点时，才单独写 <Picture N> is the first frame of [Shot 1] …
3. summary：一段英文，以 [reference generation] / [video editing + …] 这类方括号任务类型开头，只用已定义标签。
4. retention_analysis：每个标签一行，标记只能是 fully_preserved / partially_preserved / attribute_transfer / weak_reference；音频用 fully_copy / partially_copy / reference / weak_reference；这里**不写** (Sx)。
5. detailed_description：[Shot 1] 不带时间戳，第 2 镜起 `[Shot N] At MM:SS.mmm, …`；每镜写构图、主体外观与位置、环境光线、动作、镜头运动、同期声、参考内容出现时点；说话人给稳定编号 <Subject N> (Sx)；生成类任务 350–500 英文词。
6. overall_soundscape：全片环境声与物理声；对白与逐镜同步音留在 detailed_description。
7. non_diegetic_music：只写给观众听的配乐（乐器/速度/动态），没有写 N/A。
8. 总时长与请求秒数一致（H3 支持 4–15 秒）；不要写成剧情梗概。
